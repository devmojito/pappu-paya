About Pappu Paya

Bombay's bold, no-nonsense kitchen — now in Abu Dhabi

Born from the lanes of Bombay, Pappu Paya brings honest, full-flavoured Indian cooking to Abu Dhabi: slow-simmered Paya at dawn, hand-marinated Tandoori through the night, and Biryani that tells you where it's from in the first bite.

From our daily Paya and Nihari to the irreverent Padosi Ka Murga, Galli Ka Bakra and Macchiwali Aali sections, we cook the way Bombay eats — bold, generous, and unapologetically itself.
